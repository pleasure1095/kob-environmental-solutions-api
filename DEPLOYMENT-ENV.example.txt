MONGODB_URI=mongodb+srv://<db-user>:<db-password>@<cluster-host>/<database>?retryWrites=true&w=majority
APP_NAME=KOB Environmental Solutions
APP_HOST_NAME=https://YOUR-FRONTEND.netlify.app
JWT_KEY=<generate-a-long-random-secret>
PORT=(set automatically by Render; do not hardcode)

Frontend Netlify environment variables:
REACT_APP_SERVER_ENDPOINT=https://YOUR-BACKEND.onrender.com
REACT_APP_APP_NAME=KOB Environmental Solutions
REACT_APP_SHORT_NAME=KOB
REACT_APP_SLOGAN=
REACT_APP_EMAIL=

SECURITY: Do NOT put OpenAI, Stripe, Tap, SMTP, or other private secret keys in REACT_APP_* variables. CRA embeds REACT_APP_* values in public browser files. The current frontend includes a browser-side OpenAI key usage and must not be given a private production key.
